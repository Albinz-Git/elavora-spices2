About Elavora Spices

Luxury from nature: premium Indian spices, selected from the hills of Kerala and packed fresh.

Spices that smell like the hills

Elavora Spices brings premium Indian spices from Kerala to your kitchen. We begin with green cardamom and black pepper, two of Idukki’s most celebrated crops, and we are growing the range from there.

Every batch is carefully selected, handpicked for purity and packed fresh, in jars and pouches from 100 g to 1 kg. Each pack carries a clear label with what is inside, how much, how to store it and how long it stays at its best.

Our promise is written on every pack: pure spices, rich aroma, timeless taste.
